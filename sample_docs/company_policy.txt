MarketCast Company Policies

REFUND POLICY:
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, only store credit will be issued. Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded. To request a refund, contact support@marketcast.com with your order number.

REMOTE WORK POLICY:
All employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week. On Tuesdays and Thursdays, all team members must be in the office for collaboration days. Remote work from outside the country requires manager approval at least 2 weeks in advance.

VACATION POLICY:
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation days do not roll over to the next year. Employees must submit vacation requests at least 5 business days in advance. During peak season (November-December), vacation requests may be limited.

EXPENSE POLICY:
Business expenses over $50 require manager pre-approval. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Flights must be booked economy class unless the flight exceeds 6 hours. All expense reports must be submitted within 14 days of the expense.

HIRING PROCESS:
Our hiring process consists of 4 stages: resume screening, phone interview (30 min), technical assessment (take-home, 3 days to complete), and final panel interview (1 hour). We aim to complete the entire process within 3 weeks. All offers include a 90-day probation period.

SECURITY POLICY:
All employees must use two-factor authentication on company accounts. Passwords must be at least 12 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Report any security incidents to security@marketcast.com within 1 hour.
